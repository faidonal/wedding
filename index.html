The finer points

Everything you need to know

A few more details to help you plan your day with us — more will follow closer to the date.

Dress Code

Country formal. Think floaty dresses, linen suits and comfortable shoes — the reception spills onto grass in the evening.

Bring a layer; barn evenings can turn cool.

Accommodation

We've held a block of rooms at The Wheatsheaf Inn, a 10-minute drive away, under "Alex & Jordan."

Camping on-site is also available — let us know if you'd like a pitch.

Gifts

Your presence is truly the present. If you'd like to give something, we've set up a honeymoon fund — details on the RSVP form.

Children & Plus-Ones

We love your little ones, but our day is adults-only, with the exception of immediate family.

Plus-ones are noted on your invitation — just let us know who's joining.

How to get there

By Car

Hartwell Barn, Fossway, Stow-on-the-Wold, Gloucestershire GL54 1AA.

Free on-site parking is available in the north field — follow signs from the main gate. Please arrange a designated driver or taxi for the evening, as country lanes are unlit.

By Train

The nearest station is Moreton-in-Marsh (20 minutes by taxi), with regular trains from London Paddington.

Let us know your arrival time on the RSVP form and we'll do our best to help coordinate lifts from the station.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive from 3:30pm and be seated by 3:50pm — the ceremony begins promptly at 4:00pm.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes — the barn and orchard are step-free, with accessible parking near the entrance. Let us know if you need anything specific.

Can I bring a plus-one?

If a plus-one has been included, it will say so on your invitation and on the RSVP form.

What about dietary requirements?

There's a space for this on the RSVP form — just let us know and we'll make sure the kitchen is aware.

Is there parking on site?

Yes, free parking is available in the north field, a short walk from the barn.

What's the weather backup plan?

If it rains, the ceremony moves into the barn — either way, we'll be married by 4:30pm.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We'd love for you to be present rather than behind a screen — we'll have a photographer, and phones can come out from the reception onward.

By when should I RSVP?

Please reply by 1 April 2027 using the form below so we can finalise catering and seating.
