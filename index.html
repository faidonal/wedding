Dress Code

Country formal. Think a bit fancy, summer dresses, linen suits and of course, Kilts encouraged.

Some things you might want consider bringing: a change of comfy shoes (there will be games and a Ceilidh) and layers if the nights are looking cold. We are hoping to be as outside as possible. Please check the weather forecast in the days before.

Accommodation

We are very near Stansted Airport, so there are lots of hotels and short term rentals in the area. We recommend booking early as it is a UK weekend holiday.

Camping on-site is also available but let us know in your RSVP if you'd like a pitch so we can organise.

We are a 30 minute train ride from central London too, if you wish to stay there. See "How to get there" for more information.

Gifts

Truely, and cliché, but your presence is the only gift we need so please no presents!

Children & Plus-Ones

We’re keeping our celebration to close friends and family, so invitations are addressed by name and only those listed are invited.

For those with kids, as much as we love them and will miss them, we’re keeping the day adults only.

By Car

Free on-site parking is available in the north field — follow signs from the main gate. Please arrange a designated driver or taxi for the evening, as country lanes are unlit.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive from 3:30pm and be seated by 3:50pm — the ceremony begins promptly at 4:00pm.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes — the barn and orchard are step-free, with accessible parking near the entrance. Let us know if you need anything specific.

Can I bring a plus-one?

If a plus-one has been included, it will say so on your invitation and on the RSVP form.

What about dietary requirements?

There's a space for this on the RSVP form — just let us know and we'll make sure the kitchen is aware.

Is there parking on site?

Yes, free parking is available in the north field, a short walk from the barn.

What's the weather backup plan?

If it rains, the ceremony moves into the barn — either way, we'll be married by 4:30pm.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We'd love for you to be present rather than behind a screen — we'll have a photographer, and phones can come out from the reception onward.
